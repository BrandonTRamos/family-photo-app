# Family Photo App

A private, shared place where a family keeps its photos and videos, older memories and new moments alike, and browses them by date and tags.

## Language

**Archive**:
The single shared collection holding every photo and video the family has added.
_Avoid_: Library, album (for the whole collection), gallery

**Album**:
A named group of items from the Archive, made by a family member (for example, "Lake trip 2019"). An item can be in any number of Albums and always remains in the Archive; deleting an Album never deletes its items.
_Avoid_: Folder, collection, story

**Capture date**:
The exact date an item was taken, read from the file or entered by a family member for an Undated item. It places the item on the Timeline. There are no approximate dates.
_Avoid_: Upload date, approximate date, era

**Undated item**:
An item with no Capture date. It appears in the Undated section instead of on the Timeline.
_Avoid_: Unknown date, missing date
