# Family Photo App

A private, shared place where a family keeps its photos and videos, older memories and new moments alike, and browses them by date and tags.

## Language

**Archive**:
The single shared collection holding every photo and video the family has added.
_Avoid_: Library, album (for the whole collection), gallery

**Item**:
A single photo or video in the Archive. It is ready once processed; only ready Items appear on the Timeline, in Undated, in Albums or in search.
_Avoid_: Asset, media, file, post

**Recently deleted**:
Where an Item goes when its uploader deletes it. Only the uploader sees it there and can restore it, with its Tags and Albums. After 30 days it is deleted for good, files included. Only the uploader can delete an Item.
_Avoid_: Trash, bin, archive (for deleted items)

**Album**:
A named group of items from the Archive, made by a family member (for example, "Lake trip 2019"). An item can be in any number of Albums and always remains in the Archive; deleting an Album never deletes its items.
_Avoid_: Folder, collection, story

**Timeline**:
The Archive's dated items in order, newest first, grouped by year, then month, then day.
_Avoid_: Feed, stream, history

**Capture date**:
The exact date an item was taken, read from the file or entered by a family member for an Undated item. It places the item on the Timeline. There are no approximate dates.
_Avoid_: Upload date, approximate date, era

**Undated item**:
An item with no Capture date. It appears in the Undated section instead of on the Timeline.
_Avoid_: Unknown date, missing date

**Tag**:
A word or short phrase describing what an item shows (for example, "beach" or "birthday cake"). Any family member can add or remove one.
_Avoid_: Label, keyword, category

**AI tag**:
A Tag generated automatically from a photo's contents and marked as such. Videos never get AI tags.
_Avoid_: Auto tag, machine label

**Family tag**:
A Tag added by a family member.
_Avoid_: Manual tag, user tag
